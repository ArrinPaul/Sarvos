# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders, CTOs and engineering leads at organizations with complex or high-reliability software needs. They are deciding whether to hire an independent studio, and they judge competence quickly from how the site itself is built. (Confirmed: audience is prospective clients, not recruits.)

## Product Purpose

SARVOS is an independent software and technology studio. The site exists to win client projects. The primary conversion is the project inquiry form at /contact.

## Positioning

Direct engineer-to-client collaboration with no account-manager layer. Full IP ownership from day one. Every sprint ends in verified, deployed software.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4, Geist, light and dark themes. Pages: home, services (+detail), work (+case study), about, insights (+article), contact. Existing Vitest and Playwright suites must keep passing. The contact email is a placeholder.

## Brand Commitments

Monochrome Swiss-modernist identity (user chose to evolve it, not replace it): Geist, black and white, editorial restraint. No accent colour.

## Evidence on Hand

Real content lives in src/content (projects, services, process, faq, insights). No customer logos, testimonials or benchmarks exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Prove engineering rigor through the craft of the site itself.
2. One clear action: start a project.
3. Motion is refined and purposeful, under 300ms for UI and slower only for editorial reveals.
4. Honest content: no invented claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Keyboard focus is always visible. Hover effects are gated to hover-capable pointers.
