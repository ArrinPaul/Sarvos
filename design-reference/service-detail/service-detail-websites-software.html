[ 02 / IDENTIFIED BOTTLENECKS ]

Structural failures we systematically diagnose and eradicate.

SYS.DIAG // 01 ARCHITECTURE

Fragile Monolithic Platforms

Brittle plugins, bloated dependencies, and sluggish load times slowing release velocity. Security vulnerabilities compound across unmaintained legacy packages.

↳ FIX: Decoupled edge-rendered frontends with isolated micro-services.
SYS.DIAG // 02 OPERATIONS

Disconnected Internal Workflows

Teams manually shuttling data across spreadsheets because off-the-shelf SaaS doesn't match internal operations, creating data drift and administrative drag.

↳ FIX: Unified administrative control planes and deterministic webhook pipelines.

Low conversion and poor engagement caused by sluggish mobile interactions, cumulative layout shifts, sub-par Core Web Vitals, and unresponsive customer journeys.

Systems left by previous vendors without readable code, test suites, or clear deployment documentation, leaving engineering teams terrified of shipping breaking changes.

↳ FIX: Strict TypeScript contracts, end-to-end integration tests, and runnable runbooks.

[ 05 / CLIENT READINESS CHECKLIST ]

High-signal brief preparation.

To expedite architectural discovery and accurate engineering estimates, we encourage prospective clients to gather these five operational vectors before submitting an inquiry.

NOTE: Incomplete briefs are still welcome for exploratory architectural workshops.
01

Clear Statement of the Problem

What concrete structural bottleneck, performance failure, or missed revenue opportunity is forcing this build? Avoid describing the desired solution; define the operational pain point.

02

Key User Personas & Workflows

Who are the primary operators using this software daily, and what exact sequential tasks must they complete with minimum friction?

03

Existing Technical Constraints

Are there legacy databases, third-party REST/SOAP APIs, on-premises network requirements, or mandatory enterprise authentication (Okta, Azure AD) standards?

04

Target Milestones & Launch Window

When must the system be deployed to production staging or live customer hands? Is there a fixed market event, funding round, or contractual deadline?

05

Decision Stakeholders

Identify the primary technical counterpart and the executive product decision-maker empowered to approve technical architecture and sprint deliverables.
